Twelve events, as the papers told them

Each collection gathers pages printed in the days and weeks after a major event, from many newspapers and places. Every collection has a short historical overview, notes on what to watch for when reading the coverage, and starter questions.

The overviews give background so you can judge the sources. They are not quoted from the newspapers and are kept apart from search results.
