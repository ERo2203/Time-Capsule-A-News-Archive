India archive

India's gazettes and newspapers, across two centuries

Everything we could find on the Internet Archive: the Gazette of India and every state gazette, Bombay's English papers from 1811, Tamil and Telugu periodicals, and recent e-paper editions. All of it is catalogued by year below and can be searched in full text, in English or Indian scripts. A decade-by-decade sample of the public-domain material is also part of this archive's own corpus, so you can ask it questions on the main search and get quoted, cited evidence.

Newspapers

Gazettes

A gazette is the government's official journal: acts, rules, appointments, land acquisitions and public notices take legal effect by appearing in it. They record the working of the state in great detail, from colonial provincial governments to today's union and state governments, often in two languages.

About this catalog

  • Counts come from the Internet Archive's search service when this site was built; the Archive keeps growing, so the live search may find more.
  • Sampled here means public-domain issues (newspapers published before 1931, and government gazettes, which are official legal texts) have been copied into this archive's corpus at a few issues per decade. Link only means the material is catalogued and linked but its text is not copied, usually because it may still be in copyright.
  • Rebuild the catalog with timecapsule india-catalog, search from the terminal with timecapsule india-search "query" --scope gazettes, and pull more issues into the corpus by raising per_decade in corpus/collections.json.
